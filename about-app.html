About RootsTalk

RootsTalk brings trusted agricultural knowledge closer to every farmer.

A digital platform that connects farmers with agricultural organisations, experts, facilitators and input dealers — helping them access the right guidance and support throughout the crop cycle.

Open RootsTalk → For organisations →

RootsTalk is a digital platform that connects farmers with agricultural organisations, experts, facilitators and input dealers — helping them access the right guidance and support throughout the crop cycle.

Advisories

Guidance throughout your crop journey

Farmers can subscribe to crop advisories created by agricultural universities, research institutions, companies and other recognised organisations.

These advisories guide farmers from land preparation and sowing to crop management and harvest, with practices suited to the crop, farm conditions and stage of cultivation.

RootsTalk helps farmers know what to do, when to do it, how to do it, and what inputs may be required.

Diagnosis

Help when your crop has a problem

RootsTalk helps farmers identify crop-health problems through a guided diagnosis system. Farmers can also use images and AI-assisted diagnosis where appropriate.

Once a problem is identified, RootsTalk provides management recommendations from the organisation whose advisory the farmer follows.

Expert Help

Ask an Expert

Agriculture cannot always be reduced to an advisory.

Farmers can raise questions, share photographs, voice messages and text notes, and seek help from agricultural experts associated with their organisation.

Inputs

From advice to action

When an advisory or crop-health recommendation requires an input, RootsTalk helps the farmer raise a Purchase Order.

The order can reach input dealers directly or through facilitators who can help procure and deliver the required inputs.

This helps connect agricultural advice, timely action and access to inputs in one continuous journey.

Ecosystem

Built around relationships

RootsTalk does not seek to replace the people who already support agriculture. It helps them work together better.

  • Organisations create and manage knowledge.
  • Experts help farmers solve problems.
  • Promoters stay connected with farmers.
  • Facilitators help turn advice into action.
  • Dealers fulfil input requirements.

And the farmer remains at the centre of it all.

Languages

Knowledge that speaks your language

RootsTalk is designed for farmers across India. Advisories and interactions can be made available in farmers' own languages, making agricultural knowledge easier to understand and use.
